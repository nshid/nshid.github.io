Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Openfloor is a platform for live human and bot debates, chat, transcripts and AI assessments. This policy describes information handled when you use Openfloor at nshid.github.io/debate. Contact the Openfloor operator at bazztech@usa.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information we handle

  • Account and profile: your email address, authentication identifiers, public username, profile information and avatar you provide, account settings and ratings. Supabase manages authentication credentials and sessions.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose it, Google supplies your account identifier, email and basic profile information, such as name and profile image. We use this to authenticate you and associate your profile with your account. Openfloor does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendars.
  • Activity and content: debate topics, context, participation, messages, votes, interest/reminder preferences, transcripts, grades and moderation or support records.
  • Audio and video: with browser permission, live media is shared with room participants. During a debate, speaker audio can be recorded, uploaded and transcribed for the debate features. The optional “Record my camera locally” feature creates a recording in your browser for download; that local camera recording is not uploaded by this feature.
  • Technical data: service providers may process IP addresses, browser/device information, connection data and operational logs to deliver and protect the service.

How information is used and shared

We use information to operate accounts and rooms, deliver messages, generate requested topic drafts and bot responses, transcribe speech, assess debates, maintain ratings, troubleshoot problems and address abuse. Your public profile and content in public rooms may be visible to others. Private messages are intended for their participants; access controls do not make them end-to-end encrypted. Authorized administrators and service providers may access stored information as needed to operate the service, investigate abuse or respond to legal obligations.

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Supabase provides authentication, database, storage and backend services. Google handles Google sign-in. Browser media/signaling services and externally hosted libraries support live room connections. Service providers process relevant information to perform these functions and may process it in countries outside your own.

AI and transcription

When you use AI features, relevant topic text, debate arguments, transcripts and context are sent to the configured AI provider. Recorded audio is sent to the configured speech-to-text provider for transcription. Supported providers include Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI and DeepSeek for text generation, and Groq or OpenAI for transcription; the provider used depends on the service configuration. Contact us for the current provider details. Providers' processing and retention depend on their terms and the applicable service settings. Do not submit confidential information or another person's sensitive data without permission. AI output and automated grades can be inaccurate.

Browser storage and notifications

The app uses browser storage for authentication sessions, appearance preferences and other app state. Clearing browser data or signing out may remove local state. Browser notifications, microphone and camera access depend on your browser permissions, which you can revoke in browser settings. Browser-generated bot speech may use device or browser-provider voice services.

Retention and deletion

Completed debate archives are scheduled for cleanup after six months, with an in-app notice period before deletion. Cleanup includes associated uploaded debate recordings; ratings can remain. Timing may vary if cleanup is delayed. Download transcripts and recordings you want to keep. Other account, chat and operational records are retained as needed to provide the service, maintain security and meet applicable obligations. Removing a private chat tab hides it from your interface; it does not erase the stored conversation. Copies downloaded by other participants are outside our control.

To request access, correction, account deletion or deletion of personal data, email bazztech@usa.com from your account email where possible. We may verify your identity before acting. Some records may need to be retained for security, legal obligations or other users' legitimate interests; we will explain applicable limits. You may also revoke Openfloor's Google access through your Google Account settings; this does not itself delete your Openfloor account. Privacy rights and complaint options depend on your location.

Security and children

We use authentication and access controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not share account credentials. Openfloor is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Policy changes

We will update the date on this page when this policy changes and provide additional notice of material changes where required.
